# Fees and Charges

## Monthly maintenance fees
- Savings accounts: Rs 150 per month if the balance is below Rs 10,000. Free otherwise.
- Current accounts: flat Rs 500 per month.
- Fixed deposits: no maintenance fee.

## Transfer fees
- Transfers between NestBank accounts through the mobile app or web: free by default.
- Transfers initiated at a branch: an additional Rs 100 per transfer.
- RAAST instant payments to other Pakistani banks: free for customers. Settlement is instant, usually within seconds.
- SWIFT international transfers: Rs 2,500 flat fee plus 0.1% of the transfer amount. Settlement takes 1 to 3 business days.
