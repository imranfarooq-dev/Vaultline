# Personal and Auto Loans

## How to apply
Start a loan draft in the app. You can fill it in over several steps and undo changes. When you submit, the application is reviewed automatically by three desks: the credit desk, the affordability desk and the compliance desk.

## Eligibility and decision rules
- Loan term: between 3 and 84 months.
- Credit score: applicants with a score below 600, or with any past default, are rejected. Scores from 600 to 679 are considered borderline and go to manual review even if everything else passes.
- Affordability: the monthly instalment must not exceed 40% of monthly income. Instalments are calculated at an illustrative 20% annual rate.
- Collateral: loans above Rs 2,000,000 require collateral worth at least 50% of the loan amount.
- Compliance: loans above Rs 5,000,000, or for restricted purposes such as crypto, gambling or speculation, always go to a senior officer for manual review.

## Possible outcomes
APPROVED, REJECTED or MANUAL_REVIEW. The decision log explains which desk made which finding.
