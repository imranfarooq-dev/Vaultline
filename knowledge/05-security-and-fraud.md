# Security and Fraud Protection

## Automatic fraud screening
Every transfer is screened against fraud rules before money moves. Default rules:
- Mobile transfers above Rs 50,000 are blocked for review.
- Transfers above Rs 10,000 made between midnight and 5 AM are blocked for review.
Blocked transfers return the error FRAUD_SUSPECTED. Customers should call the helpline to verify and release the payment.

## Account freezes
The bank may freeze an account if suspicious activity is detected. A frozen account can still RECEIVE deposits, such as salary, but cannot make withdrawals or transfers until it is unfrozen.

## Closing an account
An account can only be closed when its balance is exactly zero. Withdraw or transfer the remaining balance first. A closed account cannot be reopened.
